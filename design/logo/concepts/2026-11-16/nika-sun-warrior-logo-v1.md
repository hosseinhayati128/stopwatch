# Nika Sun Warrior logo concept

Created 2026-11-16 with the built-in image-generation tool, then refined with the same tool.

File: `nika-sun-warrior-logo-v1.png` (1254 × 1254 PNG with alpha).

## Design

The existing Nika theme's gold Sun Warrior becomes a dancing central figure, carrying a clock shield inside a navy sun-and-stopwatch medallion. Cream cloud curls echo the theme's mural. The references were the project's `warrior-reference-flat.png` and `controller-art-reference.png` in `StopwatchOverlay.Desktop/Assets/Nika/`.

This is a standalone design concept. Application artwork and references were not replaced. Visual composition and PNG alpha were inspected; the image corners are fully transparent. Actual 16 px and 24 px tray readability has not been validated, and the figure's fine details may need simplification for those sizes.

## Initial generation prompt

```text
Use case: logo-brand.
Task: transform the Sun Warrior artwork in image 1 into ONE polished standalone logo for the "Stopwatch Overlay" desktop app, based specifically on its "Nika's Chronicle" theme.
Image 1 is the artwork to redesign: a joyful dancing Nika Sun Warrior with flame-like flowing hair, raised spear, circular shield, bent legs and swirling clouds, rendered in antique gold.
Image 2 is a supporting STYLE AND COLOR REFERENCE ONLY: the app theme, deep ocean navy, warm brass gold, parchment cream, subtle old nautical craftsmanship. Do not reproduce any interface, screenshot, text or skull from image 2.

Create a SINGLE centered app logo on a square canvas, not a concept board. A bold deep ocean navy circular medallion #032437, with a strong antique gold #C49A51 outer ring, a small simple stopwatch crown at twelve o'clock, and eight restrained broad sun-ray tips around the outside. The ring must clearly read as both a sun and a pocket stopwatch. Keep the outline clean and memorable.
Within the medallion place a beautifully balanced simplified GOLD SILHOUETTE of the dancing Sun Warrior from image 1: flowing flame hair, joyful arched profile, raised arm with spear, one bent raised knee and the other bent leg. Preserve the iconic energetic pose and its sun-god personality. Reduce the illustration to bold deliberate connected masses with generous negative spaces, about 60 percent fewer small details than the original. It should feel like a crafted crest with the clarity of a real logo, not a full mural.
The warrior's round shield is a small elegant clock face: navy cutout face, two thick warm-parchment clock hands, and only four substantial cardinal hour markers. No numbers. The clock shield gives a clever direct association with time tracking. Keep the shield clearly secondary to the whole silhouette and harmonious, not a second competing badge.
Allow just TWO broad curling cloud shapes integrated into the lower left and upper right of the internal composition, echoing Nika's cloud motifs. Avoid thin filigree. No extra surrounding particles.
Style: refined flat emblem, strong silhouette, crisp controlled curves and edges, minimal hand-engraved character in the shape language ONLY, restrained 3-color palette of navy, antique gold and a little parchment #FFF1D9. NO distress texture, NO bevel, NO metallic reflections, NO gradients, NO drop shadow, NO 3D, NO pixel art.
Composition: logo fills approximately 80 percent of the image with comfortable even transparent outer margins. Navy medallion interior is solid opaque navy; everything OUTSIDE the emblem is genuinely transparent, not a white rectangle or checkerboard drawing. Balanced and visually centered. One logo only, front-on and flat, clean high-resolution edges.
No text of any kind, no wordmark, no labels, no watermark, no devices or UI mockup, no additional icon samples.
```

## Refinement prompt

```text
Use case: precise-object-edit.
Edit the supplied Nika-themed stopwatch logo. Preserve its essential design: antique gold dancing Sun Warrior with raised spear and flame hair, small navy clock shield with cream hands, two cream clouds, navy circular medallion, golden sun rays, and stopwatch crown. Preserve the same palette and recognizable pose.
Change only finishing and framing: clean the complete outer contour and give the emblem 12 percent truly transparent margin on all four sides. Remove ALL detached stray pixels, red or yellow fringe, colored halo, speckles and residual background outside the logo. The only nontransparent pixels must belong to the single emblem itself, with clean antialiased outer edges. Make the medallion's navy interior fully opaque and flat, not textured. Make the gold and cream areas fully opaque, with clean solid fills rather than mottled or translucent patches. NO checkerboard drawn into the image, no solid rectangular background, no shadow or glow.
Keep the symbol large and crisp, centered in a square image. No text, no added elements, no layout or concept board. This is a clean standalone transparent PNG logo.
```
